Setup

  1. Open this page in Chrome from its https address, such as GitHub Pages. Chrome only allows Bluetooth on secure web pages.
  2. Close Zwift on every device, so it doesn't take the trainer or the Click.
  3. Put the chain on the 50 ring.
  4. On the Fenix, open Bike Indoor, hold MENU, choose Smart Trainer Options › Free Ride, then start the timer. The watch records the ride and this page sets the resistance.
  5. Tap Trainer and pick your KICKR. Press + on the right Click to wake it, then tap Click and pick Zwift Click. Tap Heart rate if you want it here too.
  6. Press + for a harder gear and − or B for an easier one. Use the Road buttons to add a climb.

For Garmin Coach workouts, leave the trainer disconnected here and let the Fenix control it. Outside Zwift, the left Click (−) only works for about a day after the Zwift app last unlocked it. The right Click works on its own.
